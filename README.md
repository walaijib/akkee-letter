# AKKEE — Local Fire

### Join us in building a better food system for everyone
### ร่วมกันพัฒนาระบบอาหารที่ยั่งยืน

---

At AKKEE, we believe that what ends up on the plate — and in the glass — should honor the people who grow it. This letter is an invitation to the Thai F&B community: chefs, restaurant owners, beverage makers, farmers, and producers who feel the same tension we do.

The system makes carefully grown local food harder to access than processed products shipped from the other side of the world. We think that needs to change. And it cannot be done alone.

This is not a campaign. It is not performative sustainability. It is a real conversation — and we are asking who wants to be part of it.
